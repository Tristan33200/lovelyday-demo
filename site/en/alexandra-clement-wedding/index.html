Wedding · Domaine de la Dame Blanche, Grignols · 2025

Alexandra & Clément

Alexandra & Clément imagined a wedding that was truly theirs: elegant, vintage, in mocha tones and, above all, full of surprises. At Domaine de la Dame Blanche in Grignols, surrounded by 100 loved ones, every moment had its own flavour.

It all began with an overwhelming civil ceremony: the bride's father, mayor of the village, married his daughter where it all began. A suspended moment, full of love and pride.

Then came the mystery… Guests did not know the reception venue until the day itself. You either followed the couple or hopped on the shuttle. And then: the wow effect. Total surprise, and smiles all round.

The party flowed without pause: group photos full of laughter, with personalised labels and guests called by country names, a gift bar designed as one more thoughtful touch, and a main-course service turned into a real show, with music, energy and sparklers in the room.

The night went up a notch with a live band, wild best men tossing the groom into the air, and a bartender in rhythm serving signature cocktails created by Alexandra and Clément.

A vibrant, generous wedding, full of love and joyful madness. Powerful, genuine and truly unforgettable memories.
