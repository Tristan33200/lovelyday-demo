Why hold your wedding in the Bordeaux region?

If you haven't yet found the place that perfectly reflects the wedding of your dreams, you are in the right place. The Bordeaux region is known for its prestigious ceremony venues, which express romance in all its forms and offer the ideal setting to get married in an atmosphere that matches your personality.

Getting married in the Bordeaux region means choosing an authentic, romantic and elegant celebration. Let yourselves be charmed by this dream destination for an unforgettable moment. Its natural setting and architectural richness promise to make your celebration memorable and your photos sublime. Let's discover together what makes the Bordeaux region an exceptional place.

Tempted by the authentic landscapes of Bordeaux's wine châteaux?

Let's start with our iconic wine châteaux and their natural, idyllic settings, which will give you unique wedding photos thanks to their vast vineyards and breathtaking panoramas.

You can enjoy the different seasonal colours of the forests and parks in a rustic atmosphere, close to nature.

Historic heritage châteaux will add romance and a regal touch to your photos.

Top 11 of the most beautiful châteaux and estates in the Bordeaux region

This sumptuous reception room of stone and wood awaits you at Château de Gassies in Latresne.

On an estate with an Italian air, the Galerie Cedrus welcomes you in Saint-Pey-de-Castets in a serene, romantic atmosphere.

Amaze your guests in this magnificent, warm reception room with its solid oak floor, stone walls and exposed beams at Château de la Garde in Martillac.

For a country-chic atmosphere of stone and wood, stop at the Domaine de Le Herre in Aillas.

For a grand wedding in an extraordinary building with authentic character in the heart of Grignols.

For a country atmosphere, the Domaine de Vautrait welcomes you in the heart of unspoilt nature in Cabanac-et-Villagrain.

The calm and serenity of this estate will suit a romantic wedding in the village of Saint-Pey-de-Castets.

For a timeless wedding in an 18th-century chartreuse blending modernity and authenticity, head to the village of Saint-Maixant.

Thanks to the different spaces of the châteaux, you can wander with your guests around the various parts of the property throughout the ceremony.

To make the most of the variety of settings, the parks and gardens are ideal for a symbolic ceremony, letting you enjoy a ceremony as close to nature as possible on a beautiful sunny day.

Raise your glass in the courtyard facing the château to admire its architecture, and savour the local cuisine of the Bordeaux region in a beautiful reception room that we will have styled to reflect you.

And finally, although it is easy to forget, the very best would be to end the evening in guest rooms, in total immersion, with a panoramic view over the property.

Inner beauty

Beyond its image as a tourist beach, getting married on the Atlantic coast will give you a sense of escape, to the poetic rhythm of the waves, with its ocean panoramas and marinas.

For an unforgettable wedding in a period château in the heart of the Landes forest, in the town of Linxe.

For an authentic atmosphere where the estate showcases the beautiful union of stone and wood, in the village of Martillac.

For a wide choice of settings, this estate will surely offer the ideal venue for your ceremony in La Roche-Chalais.

Let yourself be carried away by the romantic charm of the Bordeaux region's coastline?

This estate, with varied spaces steeped in history, will be the ideal setting for a simple, refined wedding with a country or gourmet atmosphere.

This haven of peace invites you to recharge in a natural atmosphere of water, light and pines on the Bassin d'Arcachon.

The Landes architecture of this farm in Biscarrosse offers a calm, restful setting in a magnificent landscaped park.

You can also get married in a more festive, energetic atmosphere, such as at La Cabane Bartherotte, to the sound of the waves.

Looking for a place that is different from the wedding receptions you have already attended? Then you are in the right place: there are venues you would never have thought of booking for your wedding. Let me guide you through the cobbled streets and 18th-century architecture to the unusual venue for your reception.

For travel lovers, you can opt for a nautical style with a touch of adventure by sailing aboard the Marco Polo boat. You can enjoy its magnificent views of the riverbanks, which change as the cruise goes on, transporting you into a world that reflects you.

For an unusual reception with a change of scenery, this reception room gives you a panoramic view over the racecourse and the natural, flower-filled landscape of La Teste-de-Buch.

For those looking for a more classic yet still chic and romantic reception, I recommend this luxury hotel and its urban lofts, which will welcome you with great elegance for a ceremony that lives up to your expectations.

A team always by your side

Getting married isn't always plain sailing after a long, stressful period organising the most beautiful day of your life and having to coordinate everything on the day. But don't panic: there are many dedicated, innovative local wedding suppliers to help you.

I am also here to guide you and put you in touch with quality professionals who will listen to your wishes and are ready to help you create the most romantic of ceremonies. I will stay by your side to personalise your wedding so that it reflects you, in harmony with your preferences and your theme.
