A day in the life of a wedding planner

A wedding day is often seen as a magical day, full of emotion, laughter and highlights. For the couple, everything should feel smooth, natural and effortless. Yet behind this impression of simplicity lies an enormous amount of organisation.

Being a wedding planner isn't just about “preparing a pretty wedding”. It means running a complete project, with a budget, suppliers, timings, the unexpected and, above all… a lot of logistics.

Today, I am taking you backstage to discover the preparation beforehand and what a wedding day really looks like on the day itself, particularly in a dynamic region like Nouvelle-Aquitaine.

Preparation: it all starts long before the big day

Contrary to what you might think, most of the work isn't done on the wedding day. The big day is only the tip of the iceberg. A successful wedding relies on weeks, sometimes months, of preparation.

The to-do list: the foundation of all the planning

Every wedding is a project in its own right, almost like a small business to run. Before even talking about decor, the dress or the atmosphere, the wedding planner starts by laying the foundations: understanding the couple's vision, their wishes, their constraints and their budget, then turning all of that into a clear, structured plan. This is when the support role really comes into its own, because many couples don't always know where to start or in which order to proceed.

The to-do list then becomes the indispensable everyday tool. It brings clarity, sets priorities and, above all, stops you feeling overwhelmed by everything there is to manage. Because in wedding planning, everything comes down to anticipation. You have to think about the big decisions, but also a multitude of details that may seem secondary… until they become essential.

The wedding planner uses this checklist as a common thread. It records the key steps, urgent actions, approvals to obtain and points to review, as well as long-term tasks to schedule. This lets her anticipate important deadlines, plan meetings with suppliers, follow the progress of the wedding file week after week and make sure nothing slips through the net.

This rigorous organisation is essential, because a wedding is a one-off event: there is no second chance. Everything must be ready at the right time, in the right order and, above all, without unnecessary stress for the couple. A single forgotten approval, an unsigned document or a miscommunicated detail can have serious consequences on the big day. That is precisely why the wedding planner works ahead with method, precision and a real overall vision.

In reality, this to-do list isn't just a list of tasks. It is the heart of the organisation, the foundation on which a harmonious, smooth and perfectly orchestrated wedding is built.

2. Themed decor and atmospheres

Themed weddings are booming. Couples choose themes that reflect their personality and passions. Decor, outfits and even menus are carefully selected to immerse guests in a unique world. Whether it is a journey back in time to the Roaring Twenties, a fantasy adventure in an enchanted kingdom or an exotic celebration inspired by cultural traditions from around the world, every detail is designed to create an unforgettable experience.

Finding and selecting suppliers: building the right team

One of a wedding planner's main missions is to help the couple choose the right suppliers, according to their style, their budget and, above all, their expectations. Because a successful wedding relies first and foremost on a reliable, professional team able to work together in a calm atmosphere.

This usually covers all the essential pillars of the wedding: the caterer, the photographer, the videographer, the DJ or live band, the florist, the decorator, furniture hire, the officiant, and the whole beauty side with hair and make-up.

But the wedding planner's work isn't limited to sending a list of contacts. She takes the time to compare services, check that they fit the overall project, advise the couple according to their personality and sometimes even negotiate certain items to make the most of the budget. She also makes sure every supplier fully understands the world of the wedding and the level of quality expected.

The goal is simple: to build a solid, harmonious and experienced team able to follow a precise running order and handle the day without stress. Because on the big day, everything must flow naturally, and that depends enormously on the quality of the chosen suppliers.

Creating the timeline: the ultimate guide

Once the big decisions are made, it is time for one of the most important tools in wedding planning: the timeline.

This document is essential because it shows exactly what to do, when to do it and in what order. It serves as a common thread throughout the preparations and stops the couple feeling lost or overwhelmed.

The timeline usually includes all the key steps, such as paperwork, choosing outfits, the caterer's tasting, approving the decor, sending the invitations, organising the seating plan and finalising the full running order of the big day.

A well-organised wedding is one where the couple can move forward calmly, without feeling they are racing against time. The wedding planner is there to structure each step, give a clear overview, anticipate deadlines and, above all, prevent the famous mental load that can quickly take over during the preparations.

Moodboard, set design and atmosphere: bringing a vision to life

The creative side is the stuff of dreams… but it also demands enormous precision. Because imagining the world of a wedding isn't just about choosing pretty colours or finding inspiration on Pinterest. It means building a true visual identity that is coherent, elegant and, above all, faithful to the couple's story.

The wedding planner then works on the whole atmosphere: defining the wedding style (bohemian, chic, romantic, modern, minimalist…), choosing a colour palette, imagining the floral decor, harmonising the stationery (invitations, menus, place cards), thinking about the lighting and designing the overall staging of the spaces.

Everything is studied so that the whole is smooth and perfectly balanced. The ceremony must match the drinks reception, the room the tables, the small details the large decorative elements. Nothing should feel “off-topic” or added at random.

This set design work is often invisible to guests because it looks natural once in place. Yet it is what makes all the difference to the final result, to the atmosphere and to the photos. A harmonious wedding is almost always the result of a clear vision and meticulous work beforehand.

Managing the budget: avoiding nasty surprises

The wedding budget is an unavoidable subject, and often one of the most sensitive. Many couples have an overall idea of how much they want to invest, but quickly discover that some costs are higher than expected, or that some services involve extra charges.

A wedding planner helps you stay in control. Her role is to allocate spending wisely, prioritising what really matters to the couple. She also anticipates the hidden costs that can quickly push up the bill: delivery, set-up, take-down, overtime, travel or additional equipment.

With her experience, she helps the couple make strategic choices, find the right balance between dream and reality, and stick to the budget without sacrificing style or quality. The aim isn't to cut everything, but to invest in the right places and avoid last-minute surprises.

Because a successful wedding is also one you can fully enjoy… without financial stress.

Coordination and communication: emails, calls, confirmations… on repeat

It is a part of the job that is rarely talked about, but it takes a considerable amount of time: communication. A wedding planner spends her days handling a multitude of tasks, all focused on follow-up and coordination. She answers the couple's messages, chases suppliers, confirms timings, organises meetings, sends documents, checks contracts and adjusts details as needed.

Between WhatsApp, emails, calls and video calls, the phone becomes almost an extension of her hand. Every interaction must be carefully noted and followed up so nothing is forgotten. And to keep up this frantic pace, it is not unusual for her to drink four or five teas or coffees a day, essential for staying focused and efficient.

Finalising the wedding-day schedule

As the wedding approaches, everything speeds up. The wedding planner draws up the complete running order of the big day, minute by minute, so that every moment goes perfectly. She plans the suppliers' arrival, the decor set-up, the couple getting ready, the ceremony, the drinks reception, the photos, the entrance into the dining room, the dinner, the entertainment, the cutting of the cake, the first dance and the end of the evening.

But she doesn't stop there: each supplier receives a specific schedule detailing exactly what they need to do and when. This document becomes the real key to a smooth day, ensuring that everyone works in perfect coordination and that every moment of the wedding goes without a hitch.

The big day: what does a wedding day look like backstage?

On the wedding day, everything must work like a well-oiled machine. For the couple, the goal is simple: enjoy. For the wedding planner, it is the moment when everything that has been prepared comes to life.

Morning: final check and set-up

The wedding planner arrives at the venue early. Before even saying hello, she is already in control mode, ready to check every detail. She does a full tour of the venue, makes sure the bad-weather plan B is in place, welcomes the suppliers, coordinates deliveries and oversees the set-up of furniture and decor.

This is often when the unexpected appears: a late delivery, an order error, a missing item… The wedding planner's role is to solve each problem quickly and discreetly, so that nobody notices anything and the wedding can begin perfectly.

Setting up the decor: precision and an eagle eye

Every decor detail is scrutinised. Tables are carefully arranged, flowers selected and placed to create a harmonious whole, candles lined up, the seating plan checked, guest favours placed with taste, the ceremony space optimised and the photo corner ready to welcome memories.

Every element must be beautiful, balanced and harmonious. The wedding planner adjusts, moves, corrects and refines until the last minute, with a keen eye that misses nothing. Every gesture helps turn the venue into a magical, perfectly orchestrated space.

Final stress-free checklist

Before the guests arrive, everything must be approved and every detail carefully checked. The rings are ready, the bouquet carefully arranged, the stationery checked, the ceremony music set, the microphone and sound tested, supplier timings confirmed and the photographer's position clearly defined.

This is also when the wedding planner brings out her emergency kit, with pins, plasters, tissues and stain removers… because a wedding without surprises simply doesn't exist. She anticipates every small emergency so that everything runs without a hitch, even when surprises happen.

The couple getting ready: managing the timing and reassuring

While the venue is being finalised, the wedding planner makes sure the couple stay perfectly on schedule. She checks that hair and make-up are on track, the dress is ready, the witnesses are there and, above all, that nobody panics.

On the big day, emotions run very high. In these moments, the wedding planner becomes an essential anchor: calm, reassuring and steady, able to manage the surrounding stress while supporting the couple with gentleness and professionalism. She embodies serenity at the heart of the bustle, allowing everyone to fully enjoy this unique day.

Welcoming guests and the ceremony: total discretion, maximum coordination

The guests begin to arrive and the wedding planner stays in the background, invisible but extremely attentive. She watches every detail: guest seating, the music, how people move around, the timing. Every movement and every gesture counts for everything to unfold harmoniously.

The ceremony is a key moment of the wedding. The couple's entrance, the speeches, the music, the exit and the transition to the drinks reception must be perfectly synchronised. And if something unexpected happens, she steps in discreetly, solving each problem without interrupting the magic of the moment.

Drinks reception: pace, photos and coordination

The drinks reception is often seen as a relaxing time for guests, but behind the scenes it is a strategic period. The wedding planner coordinates the caterer's service, keeps an eye on the timing of group photos, oversees any entertainment and prepares the room for dinner. It is also often the only moment when the couple can breathe a little, knowing that every detail is taken care of and everything continues to run perfectly.

Entrance, dinner and surprises

While the guests enjoy the drinks reception, the wedding planner carries out a final full check of the room. She makes sure the tables are perfect, the lighting is right, the guest favours are in place, the DJ is briefed and the caterer's timing is confirmed.

Then comes the entrance into the room, a powerful moment when the energy instantly rises. During dinner, the wedding planner continues to oversee every detail: the pace of the courses, speeches from loved ones, the witnesses' surprises, the planned entertainment and the coordination between the DJ and the caterer. Her goal is clear: avoid lulls and keep a smooth, pleasant pace for everyone.

First dance and party: magic to the very end

When the party really begins, the work isn't over. The wedding planner oversees the first dance, the arrival of the cake, the timing of key moments and the coordination of suppliers, while handling small last-minute hitches. Even when the dance floor is full and the guests are having fun, she stays alert behind the scenes, ready to step in if needed so the magic lasts until the very last moment.

End of the evening: tidying, collecting and a final mission

While the guests continue to enjoy the evening, the wedding planner is already organising the end of the event. She collects the couple's belongings, secures the gifts, packs away decor items, coordinates the suppliers and checks that the venue is spotless. Her goal is clear: that the couple have nothing to manage, so they can savour every moment until the very end, with a light and serene mind.

In short…

A wedding day may seem simple and smooth to those who take part, but behind the smiles, the happy tears and the highlights lies meticulous organisation. Timeline, supplier coordination, logistics, timing, handling the unexpected… everything is thought out in advance. The wedding planner's role is to make the day smooth, reassuring and unforgettable, so that every moment is magical. Because the true magic of a wedding is prepared long before the first “I do”.
