Wedding · Domaine de Conseillant · 25 June 2022

Maï & Rufus

Two hearts, one soul: on 25 June 2022, Maï and Rufus said “I do” in Gironde. Their celebrant-led ceremony took place, full of joy and emotion, at Domaine de Conseillant in Cubzac-les-Ponts.

With a romantic bohemian theme, their bond was palpable throughout this intense day. Beneath orchids, pampas grass and a very pared-back style, more than a hundred guests laughed, cried, danced and loved. Thanks to our trusted suppliers, we made their dream come true: becoming husband and wife!

In their words
« Alexandra took care of the decor and coordination of our wedding. She was a great support, full of good advice and truly attentive to our wishes and budget. Thanks to her experience, she thinks of every detail and knows what she is talking about. You can't imagine the amount of work behind a wedding, so don't hesitate to hire a coordinator for the day. The decor is as meticulous as it is beautiful! She made sure our wedding went exactly as we had dreamed. Thank you again Alexandra! Go for it, she's the best! »
Maï & Rufus Translated from French
