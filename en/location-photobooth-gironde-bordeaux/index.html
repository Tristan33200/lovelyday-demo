What is a photo booth?

Somewhere between a smartphone and a vintage photo booth, it is a portable photo stand on legs. It instantly captures moments of fun and joy: a few seconds later you are holding your print, made right there by the booth.

The bonus: it also makes GIFs, boomerangs and short videos. What better way to entertain your guests and keep an unforgettable memory of your wedding?

The booth is based near Bordeaux: collect it a few days before your event and return it the day after.

Why rent a photo booth?

Easy to use

Easy to transport and switch on, with an online tutorial to guide set-up.

400 prints included

Comes with 400 professional-quality prints. If they run out, every photo is shared on a drive 48 hours after the event.

Quality & personalisation

Instant 4×6 prints, classic or photo-strip format, personalised to your wedding theme.

Think props

Moustaches, glasses, masks… and a backdrop to match your theme: boho, countryside, beach.
