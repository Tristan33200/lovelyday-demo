How to choose the ideal, trustworthy wedding planner in 2025

Ah, weddings! That moment when love and logistics meet in an explosion of confetti and flowers… If you are planning your big day and the idea of handling everything from A to Z is causing you a good dose of stress, don't panic! A trustworthy wedding planner can be your precious support for a magical, stress-free day. In this article you will find all the advice you need to find a trustworthy professional able to turn your wedding dream into reality… and spare you all the organisation.

Identify your needs

Before you start searching for the ideal wedding planner, take a deep breath and take a moment to think. Ask yourself: do you need full support, or just a little help organising the day itself so you don't end up running around with a bouquet in your hands? You probably already have a clear vision of what you want, but a little brainstorming together can sometimes change everything. This will directly affect your choices and, of course, your budget.

Shortlist your favourite agencies

Looking for a rare gem? Then put on your detective hat and start with the classic sources: read wedding blogs, browse social media (hello Instagram and Pinterest!) and ask your friends or reliable suppliers (because we all know a good tip travels by word of mouth). Be selective in your search: choose a trustworthy wedding planner who matches your tastes and preferences. Do you dream of an eco-friendly wedding with bamboo garlands? A ceremony abroad where everything is more romantic and the sun shines brighter than your future spouse? Or do you want to say "I do" in a language that is close to your heart? By digging deeper, you will surely find the agency able to meet all your expectations. Who knows, the ideal wedding planner might also have a secret talent for finding the best pâtisseries in the region… Because after all, is a wedding without a proper cake really a wedding?

Look at the portfolio!

A wedding planner's experience is essential, as is the way they work. It is a bit like a first date: you need to know if it's a match! So before committing to this adventure with so many decisions to make, take a look at their portfolio. Do their photos make you want to get married on the spot, or run a mile? If you love minimalist weddings but their portfolio is full of overloaded sets and glitter, it may be wise to reconsider your choice and look for another match. However, if every page makes you say "Wow, that's exactly what I want!" and you discover lots of inspiration, then you have probably found THE ideal wedding planner!

Why hire a wedding planner for your big day?

So why hire a wedding planner, you ask? Well, let me explain!

A wedding planner takes care of all the details that can make your head spin, like a superhero of organisation: budget, suppliers, venues. No, it isn't a dream, it's reality!

Because you deserve to focus on your future spouse!

They know many suppliers, the best reception venues and, of course, the latest trends (because let's be honest, you don't have time to follow every wedding trend between searching for the perfect veil and choosing the DJ).

Finally, stress… that nasty monster that lurks around every wedding. Why give in to the anxiety of "will my guests get lost?" or "will the caterer arrive on time?" when a trustworthy wedding planner is there to handle all of it? You can finally sleep soundly while they take care of everything.

So if you are already overwhelmed by the preparations, or simply don't have the time, perhaps it is time to entrust the organisation of your wedding to someone with the superpower of making it all easier.

Take reviews into account

Previous clients' comments are a bit like a restaurant's stars: it is better to have several to avoid ending up with a wedding planner who has never seen a bouquet up close!

Testimonials are therefore a must. They are essential for knowing whether past couples felt like kings and queens on the big day and whether their experience went well. If their feedback is full of compliments and the praise is worthy of an Oscar, that is surely a good sign!

The meeting

A face-to-face meeting (or a video call if you are extremely busy) is essential. You need to feel comfortable with them. After all, you are about to entrust them with one of the most important days of your life! First impressions are often right, so follow your intuition! Do you get on well? Do you feel this person is made to organise your wedding? Do you feel you have found your new BFF after five minutes? Go for it, you are on the right track!

If, on the other hand, you have doubts and feel no connection… choose someone else!

It is perfectly normal to want to check that the communication and rapport will be there. Because once the contract is signed, you will spend months together discussing every little detail, from the invitations to the flowers and the cake.

And what about the financial side?

It is time to talk numbers, and no playing hide and seek!

Be very clear with your wedding planner, and make sure the costs are transparent. Yes, I mean every little expense, with no surprises on the invoice, like: "Ah, you thought the flowers cost €500, but actually it's €3,000"… Surprise!

EVERYTHING must be clear from start to finish. You don't want to discover at the last minute that you need to find money for the decor, do you? So choose a wedding planner you trust and ask all the necessary questions!

What is included in the quote? What are the extra costs? What are the cancellation terms? Can the decor be beautiful without us having to sell our flat?

The ideal wedding planner is the one who tells you clearly: "Here is where every euro goes, and here is how we will stick to your budget."

Your contract: a wedding or an action film full of plot twists?

The contract acts as a kind of compass for your collaboration with your wedding planner. If you don't want to end up lost in a field of confetti, take the time to read it carefully! Check the services included, the cancellation terms, etc. Ask all your questions if you have any doubts at all!

This is no time to skip steps, so make sure everything is clearly written down. Because your wedding is one of the biggest projects of your life! No room for improvisation!

Organisation

First, the organisation begins with our first meeting, where I take the time to get to know you better, understand your expectations and learn all about you. Then together we draw up the brief and the schedules, keeping you informed at every stage. The search for the ideal venue follows: Bordeaux vineyards, Médoc châteaux, a private villa overlooking the water… Together we choose your ideal venue.

Production

Next comes the production phase, where I take care of the choices, tastings and trials and the drafting of contracts with the various suppliers needed to create an unforgettable wedding. From the caterer to the DJ, the photographer to the videographer, the florist to the officiant, and hair and make-up, every detail is carefully researched with you to select the best suppliers for your wedding.

Design

Third, the design takes shape. After an in-depth discussion to learn your tastes and the style you want (chic, pared-back, country, colourful…), I create a mood board to design the styling of your wedding and create your wedding decor from A to Z.

The big day

Finally, on the big day, I am there to coordinate and oversee your wedding expertly, from the ceremony to the evening party, so you can fully enjoy this memorable day.

In short…

By following these steps, you will be ready to search for and choose your ideal wedding planner. A wedding planner will surely turn the organisation of your wedding into a fun time.

So, are you ready to trust a wedding planner to save you time and give you quality support?
