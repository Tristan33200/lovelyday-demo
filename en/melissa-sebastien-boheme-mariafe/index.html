Wedding · Domaine Le Herre · 6 August 2022

Mélissa & Sébastien

Mélissa & Sébastien travelled from afar to be with their family and marry at Domaine Le Herre on 6 August 2022. The light, the warmth, the atmosphere, the setting, the decor… I could go on forever describing this sumptuous bohemian wedding in terracotta, blue and rustic wood.

On this special day the couple put the emphasis on family, the exchange of rings and the celebrations, and I believe the challenge was met with flying colours! Discover the photographs of this sumptuous wedding…

In their words
« Living abroad, we absolutely needed a wedding planner. But how do you choose? You are entrusting one of the most beautiful days of your life to someone you don't know. So we went with our feelings. After several video calls with different planners, we chose Alexandra. What a great choice!!!! Alexandra is kindness itself. She is patient, attentive and efficient. She organised the wedding we had dreamed of. She was always available to guide us, inform us and reassure us. She is also an excellent decorator. In short, you can entrust your wedding to Alexandra with your eyes closed; we can assure you that you won't be disappointed. Thank you again Alexandra. »
