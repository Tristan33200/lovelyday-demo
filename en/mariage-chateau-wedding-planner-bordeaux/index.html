A château wedding with a wedding planner near Bordeaux

A wedding in a Bordeaux château

They trusted Lovely Day Event, a high-end wedding planner, to make their dream come true: celebrating their wedding in a Bordeaux château.

Love shines in their eyes! Let yourself be carried away by this luxurious union!

If you too dream of a fairytale wedding in a prestigious venue, don't hesitate to get in touch. I regularly work on weddings in châteaux, manor houses and grand estates in the Bordeaux region.

From coordinating the suppliers to the set design of the decor, the couple said "yes" in one of the most beautiful venues in the Gironde: Château Grattequina in Blanquefort, in the Bordeaux region.

The festivities lasted 3 days with 300 very elegant guests. A true fairytale wedding!

The old stonework was enhanced by a play of light that brought modernity to the setting. A striking contrast. And the quality of the suppliers (the caterer, the DJ, the saxophonist, the bartender, the photographer, the florist, the children's entertainers, the jazz band, the car hire…) created the perfect world for their personalities.
