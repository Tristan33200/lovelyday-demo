Wedding · Château d'Arche · 17 July 2021

Marie & Martin

Under the brilliant sun of 17 July 2021, Marie and Martin, two adopted Frenchies, were married near the vineyards of Château d'Arche in Sauternes, in Gironde. A day they will never forget…

The two lovebirds, who cannot take their eyes off each other, met during their studies and have never been apart since. Bringing together everyone they love to celebrate their wedding in France was an obvious choice.

Marie and Martin chose a religious ceremony to exchange their vows before God. Then came the celebrations, a point of honour for these two bon vivants, in a bespoke setting that reflected them: romantic and industrial. They pulled out all the stops: sunset over the vines, a starry sky, views over the wine cellars… and their parents' surprise, a fire and LED show by a fire juggler!

In their words
« We asked Alexandra to plan our wedding from A to Z. Alexandra is organised, available, honest, attentive, full of good advice, resourceful and a skilled negotiator; she will make your wedding her personal mission! From the preparations to the end of the festivities, including the brunch, everything ran like clockwork. We didn't have to worry about a thing, which let us make the most of our guests and the party. Alexandra created a wedding exactly like us, just as we had imagined it. You can trust her with your eyes closed; she is the perfect wedding planner, we could not have dreamed of better. Alex, thank you so much for everything you did to make this day unforgettable! »
