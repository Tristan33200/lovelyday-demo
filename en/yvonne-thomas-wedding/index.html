Wedding · Château Belle Époque, Landes · 6 September 2025

Yvonne & Thomas

In September 2025, Yvonne & Thomas, a Franco-German couple, crossed France to celebrate their union at Château Belle Époque, in the Landes. Conceived as a timeless interlude, their wedding unfolded over four days in an atmosphere that was elegant, convivial and deeply human. The pared-back, elegant art direction left plenty of room for meaning and emotion, at the heart of a truly international celebration. Each day came to life in carefully chosen places: from a traditional Landes hall to a beachfront restaurant, from a dinner under a glass roof to a brunch by the pool. Guests lived an intense, sincere experience, and the couple savoured every moment, fully present.

In their words
« Alexandra was far more than a wedding organiser: thanks to her calm, her kindness and her great professionalism, she allowed us to live our day to the full, without stress, enjoying every moment. Her taste and eye for detail made all the difference, and we are deeply grateful to have had her by our side. The memories of that day remain incredibly strong, and the result is magnificent. We recommend her without hesitation. »
Yvonne & Thomas Translated from French
