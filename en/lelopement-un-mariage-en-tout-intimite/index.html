Elopement: a wedding in complete intimacy

You long for an intimate wedding in the Bordeaux region, far from the usual bustle, where you and your partner can live your happiness without worrying about anyone else. That is what an elopement offers, a trend increasingly popular with couples who want to celebrate their union in an authentic, simple way.

Eloping means choosing to marry with just a few people, in an idyllic setting, setting aside formalities and big receptions, especially in the Bordeaux region. This alternative appeals for its approach centred on love and closeness, letting the couple fully enjoy their day.

Interested in this kind of wedding but not sure how to organise it? Follow me and I'll explain everything.

What is an elopement?

This concept, from the English "to elope", evokes the secret desire to run away together, or with a few close friends, to celebrate your union in an idyllic setting, with complete discretion. This romantic escape is a modern trend in the wedding world. It favours an intimate, simplified ceremony, far from the complexities of a traditional celebration. It suits you if you want to live your commitment in an authentic, profound way, focusing on what really matters to you: your feelings, your closeness and the desire to share your life with the person you love.

Rest assured, this choice doesn't mean running away from your responsibilities, but rather making a considered, personal choice. It isn't about cutting yourself off from family or friends, but about prioritising quality time with your closest loved ones. You don't have to give up the festivities, just organise the celebration more around your own wishes and your vision of the ideal wedding. You will be able to savour every moment, creating memorable experiences.

In short, it is a beautiful alternative to the traditional wedding, appealing to couples who want to celebrate their love differently. And I am here to explain how and where to organise your elopement so that it becomes your best memory.

How do you organise an elopement?

Eloping doesn't mean doing without guests altogether: in France, a legal marriage requires at least two witnesses to your love for the union to be valid.

A symbolic ceremony is particularly well suited to an elopement, as it offers great freedom to personalise your union, without the constraints of convention or family expectations. You are free to choose the place, the decor, the rituals, the music and the readings that speak to you most.

Where to elope in the Bordeaux region

The Bordeaux region, with its diverse landscapes and charming places, is the ideal setting for a romantic, memorable elopement.

Whether your passion is nature, culture or gastronomy, this region will fulfil your wishes. Discover below some of the best places to elope in the Bordeaux region.

Arcachon

This renowned seaside resort offers an enchanting setting for your elopement. Say your vows on the beach, facing the Bassin d'Arcachon and the Dune du Pilat, or choose a seaside villa or hotel. Enjoy water sports, the markets and local specialities such as oysters and canelés.

Saint-Émilion

This medieval village, both prestigious and authentic, is an exceptional place to elope. You can choose to marry in a church, a wine cellar or on a wine estate, and savour the region's famous wines and macarons. Don't miss the village's UNESCO World Heritage sites, including the Tour du Roy, the collegiate church and the catacombs.

Cap Ferret

The peninsula offers a wild, elegant setting to celebrate your love. Exchange rings on the point, facing the ocean and the lighthouse, or in a cabin or fisherman's house. It is a chance to enjoy nature, the beaches and seafood such as mussels and prawns.

Sauternes

This village is perfect for a gourmet, romantic elopement. Unite in a château, a vineyard or a guesthouse, and enjoy the local wine and foie gras. Discover the terroir, the landscapes and the authentic traditions of this village, famous for its sweet wine.

The Bordeaux region is a destination of choice for a dream elopement, offering a range of options to celebrate your love in a unique, authentic setting.

Activities to plan for your elopement

More than a simple wedding ceremony, an elopement is an intimate, personal adventure. The limited number of guests gives you the opportunity to live powerful, memorable moments in a dream setting. To enrich this unique experience, include activities that reflect you.

FL140 Lacanau

If you love thrills, dare to go skydiving. This emotional experience will perfectly symbolise your adventurous spirit. It will also give you a unique perspective on your celebration venue and a feeling of absolute freedom.

Sud-Ouest Montgolfière

For romantics, you can take a hot-air balloon ride. Fly over the ceremony venue, admire the landscapes from the sky and live a magical moment, ending your elopement with your heads in the clouds.

Le Bam

If you would rather party until the end of the night, no problem. After dinner, you can finish your evening in a karaoke bar such as Le Bam. Let yourselves be carried away by the frenzied rhythm of the music and sing at the top of your voices while enjoying delicious cocktails.

Celebrating your elopement with activities that reflect you, and sharing them with your loved ones or just your partner, will let you create memorable experiences, etched forever in your memory and your heart.

The advantages of eloping

Want a beautiful ceremony full of love, but not a big reception like in romantic films, rather an intimate getaway for two? Then an elopement is the ceremony that will suit you best.

Rather than a traditional wedding, an elopement offers many advantages.

First, a financial advantage. By limiting the number of guests, you considerably reduce the costs of the reception venue, accommodation and suppliers. This gives you the opportunity to allocate your budget to things that matter more to you, such as the honeymoon, the wedding dress or the rings that will make this day unique.

Next, an elopement is synonymous with simplicity, freeing you from the constraints of a traditional ceremony. No worries about complex preparations, protocol to follow or what others think. You can focus on what really matters: your love.

What's more, you are free to personalise your ceremony as you wish, without having to conform to social or family expectations. And above all, marrying with just a few people, or just the two of you, guarantees an intimate, authentic experience. You are free to choose a setting that inspires you, whether an idyllic beach, a majestic mountain or a city steeped in history.

In short…

Choosing to elope means choosing a modern, intimate way to celebrate your love. This trend offers you the opportunity to unite in an idyllic setting, in all simplicity, whether just the two of you or with your dearest loved ones. Every moment of your wedding becomes a faithful reflection of your couple, full of authenticity and magic.

This option isn't only romantic, it is also pragmatic. It brings considerable advantages, such as significant savings, total flexibility in the organisation and heightened emotional intensity. Don't hesitate to call on my expertise to guide and support you in this wonderful adventure.

The Bordeaux region is a destination of choice for your elopement, with its variety of enchanting landscapes and charming places. It is also a chance to discover the secrets of its exquisite wines, its rich gastronomy and its many activities, adding a touch of pleasure and originality to your celebration.

If you are drawn to the idea of an intimate, personalised wedding, an elopement is made for you. Contact me without delay to bring your dream wedding to life!
