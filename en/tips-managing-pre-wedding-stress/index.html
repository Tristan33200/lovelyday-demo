Tips for managing pre-wedding stress

Organising a wedding is a unique experience, full of joy and excitement, but it can also become a major source of stress. Between finding suppliers, choosing the venue, coordinating guests and managing the budget, it is easy to feel overwhelmed. Yet your wellbeing is the key to fully enjoying this adventure and savouring the big day. And as a wedding planner and on-the-day coordinator in Bordeaux and Nouvelle-Aquitaine, I am here to help!

Plan to manage better

As a wedding planner, I always tell the couples I work with: planning is your best ally against stress. Setting up a detailed schedule, month by month, gives you a clear view of all the preparations and lets you move forward step by step without feeling overwhelmed.

I advise you to note down all the key deadlines from the start: meetings with suppliers, payment dates, decor orders, outfit fittings, sending the invitations and the final sign-offs. This schedule becomes your common thread. It reassures you, lets you track your progress and helps you avoid last-minute oversights, which are often a source of anxiety.

Another essential point is delegation. Many couples think they have to handle everything themselves to stay in control. Yet delegating certain tasks is often what brings back peace of mind. I encourage you to entrust well-defined tasks to your loved ones or your wedding planner: logistics, communication with suppliers, coordinating invitations or keeping track of the countdown schedule.

Delegating doesn't mean letting go of your wedding; on the contrary, it gives you the mental space to enjoy this unique period. My role as a wedding planner is precisely to relieve you of the mental load, structure the organisation and let you focus on what matters: your emotions, your relationship and the pleasure of preparing this big day with complete confidence.

How to take care of yourself

During the preparations, I often remind couples that it is essential to look after themselves, even in the thick of the organisation. A wedding is a big project, but it should never take up so much room that it crowds out moments of calm and connection with yourself and your partner.

I strongly encourage you to keep moments just for you: a yoga session, a few minutes of meditation, a walk in nature or simply a relaxing evening together without talking about the wedding. These breaks are precious: they help you recharge your batteries and approach decisions with more perspective and calm.

Sometimes very little is needed. A few minutes a day of deep breathing, a break without your phone, a moment of silence or a massage may seem trivial, but these small anti-stress habits have a huge impact on your wellbeing. They soothe the mind, release tension and help you stay fully present in this wonderful adventure.

As a wedding planner, my role is also to look after your balance. A relaxed couple makes better decisions and enjoys the preparations more. Looking after yourselves isn't a luxury, it is a necessity for a calm wedding that is in tune with your emotions.

Communicating with your partner

As a wedding planner, I can't say it enough: communication is a fundamental pillar of calm preparations. A wedding is a project for two, and regularly sharing your wishes, priorities and worries avoids many unnecessary misunderstandings and tensions.

I often advise couples to schedule regular check-ins, even short ones, to review the decisions made, adjust the schedule or calmly confirm choices. This turns each decision into a shared choice, where both of you feel heard and involved.

This active communication naturally reduces the pressure and keeps you aligned throughout the preparations. It also helps you keep a light heart, savour the important steps and live this adventure together, calmly. After all, a successful wedding starts with a couple moving forward hand in hand, with listening and mutual respect.

How to manage family relationships

Family is often a precious support during the preparations, but it can also be a source of pressure or conflict. The expectations of some relatives, about the budget, the guest list or traditions, can sometimes create a lot of stress. To protect your peace of mind, it is important to set clear boundaries and communicate your decisions kindly. Involving your family in what they are passionate about, without giving them total control, lets you benefit from their help while staying in charge of your day. Some requests can be honoured, others adapted according to your priorities, so you keep the harmony and the pleasure of the preparations. As a wedding planner, I also act as a mediator. I know how to handle tensions and find compromises so that everyone feels heard, while preserving your wishes and your vision of the wedding. The aim is simple: to let you fully enjoy this adventure with your loved ones, in joy and serenity.

Relying on professional coaching

A wedding planner isn't just an organiser, but also a true emotional coach. We help you clarify your priorities and your vision of the wedding, so that every decision truly reflects your wishes. We help you step back from details that could become stressful and put small everyday tensions into perspective. We anticipate the unexpected with you by drawing up plan Bs, which considerably reduces the stress of the organisation. Thanks to this tailor-made support, you can focus on what matters, your relationship and your emotions, with the peace of mind of knowing that the logistics and all the practical aspects are in good hands.

Avoid comparisons

Social media is full of images of perfect weddings and inspiration of every kind, but it is important to remember that every couple is unique. As a wedding planner, I always advise couples to focus on what truly reflects them and on their personal wishes, rather than trying to reproduce an ideal imposed by trends. Perfection doesn't exist, and a successful wedding isn't one that follows fashions or clichés to the letter, but one that reflects your love, your personality and your story. Keeping this vision in mind, you can prepare your day with calm and authenticity, and fully enjoy every moment without unnecessary stress.

Staying flexible in the face of the unexpected

Even with meticulous preparation, the unexpected can happen: a supplier running late, unpredictable weather or a small detail forgotten. As a wedding planner, I help couples stay flexible and positive, because every situation can become a unique moment. A ceremony moved indoors can become more intimate and moving, a delay can turn into a convivial moment of sharing, and a small incident fixed at the last minute often becomes a memorable anecdote you look back on with a smile. The important thing is to stay calm and enjoy the day, knowing that every surprise can be turned into a positive experience full of emotion.

Enjoy the present moment

For me, a wedding isn't just an event to organise; it is above all the celebration of your love. My advice to the couples I work with is simple: learn to let go, to breathe and to savour every moment. Every smile, every look exchanged, every burst of laughter is far more precious than any decorative or logistical detail. Stress naturally fades when you focus on what matters: your emotions and those of your loved ones. With this perspective, you can fully live this unique day with lightness and joy, and create authentic memories that will stay with you long after the wedding.

In short…

For me, supporting couples through their preparations means above all helping them manage stress and fully enjoy this unique adventure. To achieve this, it is essential to plan carefully, delegate certain tasks to free up your mind, take time for yourselves and for your relationship, maintain clear, caring communication and handle the family's expectations gently. Finally, it is crucial to stay flexible and come back to the present moment, to your emotions and those of your loved ones. With these keys, you can approach your wedding with complete serenity, savour every step of the preparations and create unforgettable memories, with a light heart and a free mind.
