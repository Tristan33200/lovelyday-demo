Wedding · La Chartreuse des Eyres · 3 July 2021

Emilie & Aurélien

Under the brilliant sun of 3 July 2021, Emilie and Aurélien were married. After a civil ceremony the day before at the town hall of Léognan and a reunion with their loved ones, they invited everyone to Domaine de la Chartreuse des Eyres for a packed day! At the couple's request, the estate was decorated in a countryside, natural spirit.

On the programme: getting ready in the estate's beautiful rooms, then a ceremony punctuated by meaningful rituals such as the wine bottle ritual and a group song. And then… you know the rest! Celebrations with a wild dance floor until the end of the night!

In their words
« Alexandra accompanied us from the start of our project until it came to life on the day. Very creative and always full of good advice, she guided us through our choices. Surrounded by the best suppliers, she advised us while respecting our tastes and budget. To enjoy this day to the full, I can only recommend calling on her talent. »
Emilie & Aurélien Translated from French
