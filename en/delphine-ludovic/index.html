Wedding · Domaine d'Arès · 10 July 2021

Delphine & Ludovic

New parents of a one-month-old baby, marrying was obvious for Delphine & Ludovic: a way to bring their family together!

Travelling from Lille to marry in Delphine's home region, they gathered their closest family on the Friday for the civil ceremony at the town hall of Le Bouscat and a first reunion dinner, before saying “I do” on Saturday 10 July in a deeply emotional celebrant-led ceremony. Tears dried, the celebrations began! An epicurean evening outdoors under the stars. A magical, timeless moment. Two wholehearted, kind and generous people. I have no words for Delphine & Ludovic's kindness… Planning and decorating their wedding was a true pleasure!

In their words
« Alexandra is our gem ♥️ We contacted her for the full planning of our wedding on 10/07/2021. We met and it was obvious, a real emotional and intellectual crush; by the end of the meeting we knew she was the one! We lived in Lille and wanted to marry in my home region of Bordeaux. We started working together before Covid, and that complicated period was never a problem thanks to Alexandra!! Planning, decor, coordination: she did it all! With her there are never problems, only solutions! As a bonus, she reassured us when we needed it, motivated us when necessary and, above all, gave us the wedding of our dreams, because Alexandra is a magician of love!! ♥️ Kind, gentle, understanding, reassuring, organised, professional, fair, full of creativity, skilful hands, a heart of gold… the list goes on, but I'll let you discover it, because with her your wedding will be unforgettable!! »
Delphine & Ludovic Translated from French
