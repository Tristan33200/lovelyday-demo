Alexandra Ambert

Human values first

Founded by Alexandra in Bordeaux in 2014, Lovely Day Event is a French events agency specialising in the design and planning of bespoke, refined and authentic weddings in the Bordeaux region and on the Arcachon Bay.

To plan, perfect and coordinate a wedding that reflects you, I do everything to make this once-in-a-lifetime event simple, rigorous and polished down to the smallest detail.

My greatest strength as a wedding planner is the human values I put before everything else. To me, planning a bespoke wedding means listening, meeting your needs and, above all, adapting to your story as a couple.

Your story

Which moments of the day matter most to you?

Tell me how you met, your wishes, your ideas. I promise to bring ideas of my own and to accompany you with passion through such a meaningful project.

What I really do

Wedding planner: far more than an address book

A wedding means hundreds of decisions, around twenty suppliers and a single day for everything to come together. My job is to carry all of that for you, from the first meeting to the last dance.

Listen & imagine

Understanding your story, your wishes and your budget, then turning them into a clear, coherent project that truly reflects you.

Select & negotiate

Selecting the right suppliers, comparing quotes, negotiating and securing every contract. You save time, money and worry.

Design & create

Imagining the scenography, sourcing, making and installing the decor: my signature as a certified Wedding Designer.

Orchestrate the day

Conducting everything on the ground: timing, suppliers, the unexpected. You simply enjoy every moment.

Wedding creator

They call me a “wedding creator”

Truly passionate about wedding planning and wholehearted in everything I do, I make it a point of honour to give couples the details and advice that make their preparations a unique and serene time.

None of this would be possible without trusted, high-quality suppliers who match your personality. My agency works with many partners across the Bordeaux region, combining excellent service with respect for each couple's budget.

Training & experience

Certified Wedding Designer

Trained in the methods of the Wedding Academy, I manage every facet of your wedding for you, with consistency and harmony throughout.

My strength is creativity. I love decoration, the latest trends, the atmospheres created by textures and colours. I present a complete, clearly structured scenography so you can truly picture your day.
