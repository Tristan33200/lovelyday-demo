Wedding · La Ferme d'en Chon · 1 June 2024

Chloé & Romain

We never tire of reliving this colourful wedding at La Ferme d'en Chon! A day as sparkling as it was elegant, carried by a voluptuous gown, refined decor inspired by Vietnam (the country where the couple met 🏮) and a few original touches such as a burrata bar that delighted the guests. It all ended with a magical photo session on the beach, in the heart of the Landes, between golden light and sea breeze. A suspended moment, full of love and authenticity.

In their words
« Alexandra is impressive: smiling, creative, with extraordinary energy and above all very organised. We were blown away by everything she achieved for our wedding. Alexandra guided us for several months, helped us choose suppliers who matched us, advised us and listened until the very last minute. On the day, she ran the organisation masterfully and we enjoyed our wedding 150% thanks to her. The decor of the reception and the cocktail hour exceeded our expectations, and the entertainment was perfect too. Alexandra also won over our families, for whom she was a point of contact as well. You can trust her completely; choosing her was one of our best decisions. »
Chloé & Romain Translated from French
