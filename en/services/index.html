Process

Briefing

What you love, what you don't, how you met, your story, a shared passion… You have plenty of ideas for your wedding. Now it is my turn to make those dreams real, for you and your guests.

02

Guidance

Trust is built over time: regular conversations by phone, email or in person, plus my planning tools and progress documents. I become your main point of contact with every supplier.

I curate a list of trusted suppliers suited to your personalities, expectations and budget. I gather and review quotes, arrange meetings and manage contracts.

FAQ

Your questions

Is styling included?
It is included in the Signature package. With the other packages it can be added from €2,000. Design is real creative work: design book, sourcing, making, storage, installation and take-down. Keeping it separate lets you compare packages like for like.
When should we hire a wedding planner?
As soon as you start planning. The earlier you reach out, the more time we have to take care of the essentials and guide you at every key stage. I recommend a year of preparation so supplier meetings are spread out, but a wedding can be planned in 2 to 12 months.
We already have our venue. What happens next?
I take over communication with the venue from the start. After a thorough visit, I record everything needed to design the scenography and decor, and we move forward together with planning your day.
What if something doesn't go to plan on the day?
I always prepare a back-up plan, especially for bad weather. I am there on the day to coordinate any change, so you can enjoy every moment without worrying about the unexpected.
Do you plan weddings for couples living abroad?
Yes. I work with French and international couples, such as Yvonne & Thomas from Germany. We stay in touch by video call, email or WhatsApp, and your online wedding file is available around the clock.
