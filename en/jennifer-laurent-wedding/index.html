Wedding · Château de Garde · 27 May 2023

Jennifer & Laurent

After a proposal on a small beach near the tip of Cap Ferret, facing the Dune du Pilat, Jennifer and Laurent celebrated their wedding at the magnificent Château de Garde in Moulon.

The moment they had long awaited was the exchange of rings at church. The blessing was gentle and solemn; with violinists and an opera singer, the ceremony was particularly moving. Then off to the château in a beautiful convertible for an evening full of flavours and music.

Guests enjoyed a saxophonist and unique culinary experiences among the vines and by the pool. The atmosphere and the exceptional setting gave the couple exactly the energy they hoped for! Soft, amber lights were perfect to highlight their love and the warmth of this wedding.

« Alexandra is the rare gem you need to plan and live your wedding! With her professionalism, her listening and her kindness, she was a shoulder to lean on during our preparations and on the day itself, which was exceptional thanks to her.
