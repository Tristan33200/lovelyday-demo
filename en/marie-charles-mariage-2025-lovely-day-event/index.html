Wedding · Château Pontac Lynch, 4 October 2025

Marie & Charles

On 4 October 2025, Marie and Charles celebrated their wedding at Château Pontac Lynch, surrounded by 80 guests, in a retro English boudoir atmosphere: chic and full of character. Every detail told their story, from coasters personalised with their initials to a vinyl bar setting the rhythm of the cocktail hour, and even a watercolour artist offering each guest a unique portrait as a keepsake. Surprises were part of the day too, with spectacular daytime fireworks and giant Olympic-style caricature heads bringing a playful, festive touch. The generous, delicious dinner won over every guest. Despite mixed weather, the atmosphere was magical, warm and joyful: a wedding just like them, elegant and daring.
