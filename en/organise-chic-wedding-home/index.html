Can you organise a chic wedding at home?

A chic wedding at home

With the Covid-19 crisis, many couples chose to marry at home for convenience. Let's take a closer look at this trend: can you organise a chic wedding at home in the Bordeaux region?

Although the health situation sparked real enthusiasm for this kind of wedding, they are nothing new! Some couples have always chosen to marry at home, in a place dear to them, surrounded by their loved ones. While civil and religious weddings usually take place in the dedicated venues, it is entirely possible to organise a symbolic ceremony or a reception at home.

There are many reasons to marry at home: for a more intimate atmosphere, for logistical reasons, to be as close as possible to family or because of a tighter budget. Everyone is free to choose where to celebrate the most beautiful day of their life! Holding your wedding at home can have several advantages, but for everything to go smoothly, your home needs to be spacious enough to welcome all your guests. An outdoor space is also welcome so you can enjoy the festivities in the open air. So if you live in or around Bordeaux and have a garden, go for it! It is also best to favour smaller weddings, unless you have a very large estate. For the big day, it is still worth calling on outside suppliers, because a wedding at home is still a real ceremony to set up. Catering, decor, entertainment or a DJ will let you enjoy the party with more peace of mind.

As a wedding planner, I have already organised bespoke weddings at home. So I know how to handle the preparations and wedding day coordination for this kind of event. I can take care of decorating your home, anticipate the technical side and everything related to the organisation, such as finding suppliers and managing the schedule.

If the idea of organising a wedding at home in the Bordeaux region appeals to you and you'd like me to take care of all the planning, don't hesitate to get in touch!
