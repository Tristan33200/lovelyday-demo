Wedding · Villa La Tosca, Arcachon Bay · 9 October 2021

Delphine & Haythem

On-the-day coordination? Of course: I also take care of just that part if you have handled everything before. Delphine & Haythem planned their chic wedding masterfully, in the middle of Covid and postponements, so the celebration was eagerly awaited.

This wedding at Villa La Tosca, on the Arcachon Bay, famous for its waterfront setting and lush gardens, offers so many spaces and unique moments. A little corner of paradise!

Do you love authentic weddings too? Picture a private villa with luxurious suites for getting ready, a welcome drink by the infinity pool, an exclusive celebrant-led ceremony on the beach, cocktails in the warm park and dinner under the stars.

In their words
« An indispensable ally! “Enjoy it, because the day goes by so fast.” Those words took on their full meaning on our wedding day, because we were serene. Entrusting our wedding coordination to Alexandra was the key to our peace of mind. From our first meeting we knew she was the right person. Alexandra is dynamic and full of ideas thanks to her great experience. She is reassuring and knows how to manage the delicate schedule of a wedding, even during a pandemic. Smiling, radiant and attentive, she was our lifeline through that uncertain Covid period, always bouncing back and being there for us, even when we had to postpone our date. Her excellent relationships with suppliers built a true team around our wedding. Beyond a wedding planner, we met a wonderful person who listened, advised and reassured us throughout the preparations and on the day. Thank you Alexandra, and your team, for your help, advice and kindness, your professionalism, creativity, good humour, and above all your presence from dawn! We lived the most beautiful day of our lives thanks to you. Looking for the best wedding planner for the great adventure of marriage? Look no further, you have found her! »
