Marion & Charles

In May 2022, Marion & Charles said “I do” for the rest of their lives! A chic and bohemian wedding, colourful and refined: everything we love…

A day rooted in tradition, and more! After getting ready at Les Sources de Caudalie, Marion & Charles were married at the town hall of Arcachon, before promising love and faithfulness before God. They then welcomed their guests at Château Larrivet Haut-Brion to begin the celebrations…

A day full of laughter, tears and emotion. What a day!
