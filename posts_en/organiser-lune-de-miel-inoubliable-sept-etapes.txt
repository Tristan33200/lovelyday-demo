[T] How to plan an unforgettable honeymoon in 7 key steps
[D] Here are a few tips for planning a memorable, unforgettable honeymoon, from the Bordeaux region and beyond:

Whether you choose to explore <strong>exotic destinations</strong> abroad or enjoy <strong>French regions</strong> such as <strong>Bordeaux</strong>, the key is to make this trip <strong>unforgettable</strong>. From setting the <strong>budget</strong> to choosing <strong>activities</strong> and selecting <strong>accommodation</strong>, every detail counts in turning this stay into a <strong>magical</strong>, <strong>memorable</strong> moment. Here are a few <strong>tips</strong> for <strong>planning</strong> an <strong>unforgettable honeymoon</strong> step by step:</p>

[2] Which destination?
[3] <p>What is the first criterion for a <strong>successful</strong>, <strong>memorable</strong> <strong>honeymoon</strong>? The destination, of course!</p><p>Are you more drawn to idyllic <strong>beaches</strong>, snowy <strong>mountains</strong>, culturally rich <strong>cities</strong> such as <strong>Bordeaux</strong> or <strong>safaris</strong> in the wild? If you dream of a romantic setting, why not consider <strong>idyllic destinations</strong> such as <strong>Tahiti</strong>, <strong>Venice</strong> or the <strong>Fiji Islands</strong>? And if you are looking for <strong>adventure</strong>, how about discovering <strong>Lapland</strong> or <strong>Panama</strong>? Or would you rather stay in France and discover its many treasures? The options are endless, but the key is to choose a place that makes you <strong>dream</strong>. Take the <strong>season</strong> into account when choosing your honeymoon destination!</p><p>Is it <strong>spring</strong>? Where should you go for your <strong>honeymoon</strong>? I recommend <strong>Japan</strong> for the cherry blossoms, <strong>Alsace</strong> in festive mood for Easter, <strong>Costa Rica</strong> in green and blue, and <strong>Cuba</strong> under the tropical sun.</p><p>Want to leave in <strong>early May</strong>? The best destinations are <strong>Ireland</strong>, <strong>Laos</strong>, <strong>Peru</strong> and <strong>Norway</strong>.</p><p>Want to travel in <strong>autumn</strong>? Choose <strong>Greece</strong>, <strong>Morocco</strong>, <strong>Uzbekistan</strong> or <strong>Egypt</strong>. Want to go away in <strong>winter</strong>, at Christmas? That is when the northern lights of <strong>Iceland</strong> light up the sky and the snowflakes fall…</p><p>Looking for warmth and tropical beaches instead? Then opt for <strong>Greece</strong>, <strong>Australia</strong>, the <strong>Canary Islands</strong> or <strong>Malaysia</strong>. The buzz of <strong>Morocco</strong> and the tranquillity of <strong>Indonesia</strong> are also very enjoyable in winter!</p>
[4] Choose the ideal accommodation!

Want something more <strong>intimate</strong> and close-knit? Why not take a <strong>van</strong> on a <strong>road trip</strong> along the Bordeaux wine route, or <strong>abroad</strong> to discover several places? These <strong>one-of-a-kind</strong> experiences strengthen your <strong>bond</strong> and let you share extraordinary moments of <strong>intimacy</strong>.</p>
[10] Have you planned the budget for your honeymoon?
[11] <p>After the <strong>expenses</strong> of the <strong>wedding</strong>, the aim is to <strong>save</strong> as much as possible. Look at the offers on <strong>Groupon</strong>, which regularly offers <strong>discounts</strong> on accommodation and <strong>packages</strong> including many services. An <strong>all-inclusive package</strong> will help you save money and avoid carrying cash.</p><p>Want the best <strong>flight prices</strong>? So you don't miss anything, set up <strong>flight alerts</strong>.</p><p>And what if, instead of a wedding <strong>gift list</strong>, you set up a <strong>honeymoon fund</strong>? The money collected will let you treat yourselves to an <strong>unforgettable trip</strong>, and your <strong>loved ones</strong> will have helped make it possible! It is a bit like your friends and family giving you a ticket to <strong>happiness</strong>!</p>
[12] When should you go on honeymoon?
[13] <p>A piece of <strong>advice</strong>: don't fly out the <strong>day after</strong> your <strong>wedding</strong>! If you have a long flight, you risk arriving <strong>exhausted</strong>, barely able to say "hello", let alone enjoy the warm sand. Leaving some time between the two will also spare you the stress of <strong>organising</strong> the wedding and the <strong>honeymoon</strong> at the same time. So why not allow a little breathing space?</p><p>If you are in a <strong>hurry</strong>, don't panic! You can also plan a <strong>mini-moon</strong> just after the wedding. A short but intense stay! Then, once you have recovered a little from your <strong>emotions</strong> and regained your <strong>energy</strong>, you can save up for a longer <strong>honeymoon</strong> a year or two later.</p>
[14] Plan your activities
[15] <p>Think about the <strong>activities</strong> you want to do. Do you want to go <strong>hiking</strong>, take <strong>bike</strong> rides and discover the châteaux of <strong>Bordeaux</strong>, relax in the <strong>hot tub</strong> or by the fire with a good book? Don't forget that the honeymoon is also meant for <strong>resting</strong> and spending <strong>time together</strong>.</p><p>Try not to overload your <strong>schedule</strong>, alternating between <strong>sightseeing</strong> and <strong>rest</strong>! Your <strong>honeymoon</strong> probably isn't the time to cross a whole continent on foot, rushing from one <strong>visit</strong> to the next. A honeymoon is above all a time to <strong>unwind</strong>, a <strong>romantic interlude</strong>, not a stressful time!</p>
