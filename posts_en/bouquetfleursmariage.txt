[4] The everlasting bouquet: a keepsake that stands the test of time
[5] <p>Contrary to what you might think, an everlasting bouquet is not made of artificial flowers.</p><p>It is made of real, natural flowers that have undergone a specific preservation process to <strong>preserve</strong> their appearance, texture and colours for a very long time.</p><p>The everlasting bouquet answers a wish more and more couples share: to <strong>extend</strong> the emotions of the big day and <strong>keep</strong> a tangible link with one of the most beautiful moments of their life.</p><p>In a world where memories are often stored on phones or hard drives, keeping your wedding bouquet offers a <strong>different</strong> experience. [7] What is the difference between dried, preserved and artificial flowers?
[8] <p>When talking about everlasting bouquets, three terms come up again and again: <strong>dried</strong> flowers, <strong>preserved</strong> flowers and <strong>artificial</strong> flowers.</p><p>Yet these three techniques are very different.</p>
[10] Dried flowers: authentic, natural charm
[11] <p>Drying is the <strong>oldest</strong> preservation method.</p><p>It involves letting the flowers lose their water naturally so they can be kept <strong>longer</strong>.</p><p>Dried flowers appeal with their <strong>authentic</strong>, poetic and slightly <strong>vintage</strong> look. They fit perfectly into bohemian, country or romantic styles.</p><p>However, drying usually brings a few changes:</p><ul><li>Colours become softer or darker;</li><li>Petals become more fragile;</li><li>The original shape may change slightly;</li><li>Some flowers handle the process better than others.</li></ul><p>A dried wedding bouquet thus keeps all its emotional meaning, but with an appearance <strong>transformed</strong> by time.</p>
[12] Preserved flowers: the finest everlasting bouquet
[13] <p>Preservation (or stabilisation) is today the most <strong>sophisticated</strong> technique for creating a true everlasting bouquet.</p><p>The process replaces the flower's natural sap with a <strong>biodegradable plant-based solution</strong>.</p><p>Thanks to this method, the flowers keep:</p><ul><li>Their natural suppleness;</li><li>Their original appearance;</li><li>Their colour;</li><li>Their texture.</li></ul><p>The result is particularly impressive.</p><p>The flowers look freshly picked, yet they can be kept for several years <strong>without any special care</strong>.</p><p>It is the preferred solution for couples who want to keep their original wedding bouquet as <strong>faithfully</strong> as possible.</p>
[15] Artificial flowers: a decorative alternative
[16] <p>Artificial flowers are made from <strong>synthetic materials</strong> such as fabric, silk, latex or plastic.</p><p>They have certain advantages:</p><ul><li>A very long lifespan;</li><li>No maintenance;</li><li>A wide variety of designs.</li></ul><p>However, they don't carry the same <strong>emotional value</strong> as an everlasting bouquet made of the real flowers that accompanied your wedding.</p><p>The difference matters: keeping your wedding bouquet means preserving an <strong>authentic</strong> part of your story. Artificial flowers, however realistic, don't carry that <strong>memory</strong>.</p><p>That is why preserved flowers are now considered the <strong>benchmark</strong> for creating a true everlasting bouquet.</p>
