[37] Types of fabric and their characteristics
[38] <ul><li><strong>Silk, shine and elegance: </strong>soft on the skin, light, flowing, delicate and prone to stains.</li><li><strong>Lace, romance and delicacy:</strong> a refined texture with a very elegant, timeless effect, well suited to fine details (sleeves, open backs, etc.), more or less delicate depending on the type of lace.</li><li><strong>Tulle, lightness and volume:</strong> an airy effect that adds volume without weight, can be combined with other fabrics for a layered effect, and often needs an underskirt or lining because on its own it can be too sheer.</li><li><strong>Crepe, clean and simple:</strong> light, flowing, easy to wear and care for, simple yet sophisticated, but may lack "structure" in some cuts.</li><li><strong>Mikado, thick and sophisticated:</strong> stiff yet soft to the touch, ideal for voluminous dresses or sculpted cuts, heavier than other fabrics and less fluid.</li><li><strong>Cotton voile, natural and comfortable: </strong>a relaxed style, easy to care for and breathable, ideal for a summer wedding.</li><li><strong>Chiffon, flowing and very light: </strong>an airy texture with a floaty, semi-sheer effect.</li><li><strong>Brocade, luxury and rich texture: </strong>a fabric woven with gold or silver, usually with floral or geometric patterns of oriental inspiration, perfect for winter weddings or majestic venues, but heavy and hard to wear for long periods.</li><li><strong>Velvet, softness and warmth: </strong>comfortable, with an elegant and sophisticated look and a thick texture that keeps you warm, ideal for a winter wedding, but less fluid and heavier than other fabrics.</li></ul><p>So, as you will have gathered, if you are looking for an <strong>elegant</strong>, <strong>sophisticated</strong> wedding dress, <strong>silk</strong>, <strong>satin</strong> or <strong>mikado</strong> are made for you! Prefer a more <strong>flowing</strong>, <strong>romantic</strong> outfit? In that case, <strong>tulle</strong>, <strong>lace</strong> or <strong>chiffon</strong> will be <strong>perfect</strong>. Finally, if you are a <strong>laid-back</strong> bride, <strong>cotton</strong> or <strong>crepe</strong> will offer <strong>comfort</strong> and <strong>style</strong>.</p>
