[T] What suit should a man wear as a wedding guest?
[D] Just like women, male guests may wonder which suit is best suited to a wedding!
[0] <p>It's true: just like women, men sometimes find it tricky to know how to dress for <strong>a wedding</strong>. Between the different themes, colours, seasons and more, it can be hard to find your way. So today, let's look together at the <strong>dress code</strong> to follow and <strong>a few tips</strong> on how a <strong>MAN</strong> should <strong>dress</strong> for a wedding.</p>

[2] Read the invitation
[3] <p>First, it is essential to read the wedding <strong>invitation</strong> carefully, because this is where you'll find <strong>the key clues</strong> that will help you fit the theme perfectly.</p><p>As we'll see, depending on the <strong>type of wedding</strong>, there are several kinds of suit, from the most <strong>sophisticated</strong> to the most <strong>relaxed.</strong></p>
[4] The men's tuxedo (or black tie)
[5] <p>At the very top of sophistication is the tuxedo, also known as "<strong>black tie</strong>".</p><p>If the wedding you're invited to is the height of <strong>elegance</strong> and <strong>chic</strong>, this is surely the kind of suit to turn to.</p><p>To choose your <strong>tuxedo</strong> well, pay attention to a few details that seem minor at first but are in fact the <strong>key elements</strong> that will make you the best-dressed guest of the evening!</p>
[6] Suit colours
[7] <p><strong>Black</strong> or <strong>navy blue</strong> are preferred. <strong>White</strong> is also an option, but it must be specified clearly on the invitation.</p><p>Every now and then, however, you may come across the elusive <strong>"creative black tie"</strong> dress code.</p><p>With it, you can relax the traditional rules while keeping the black tie look: think of a <strong>light grey</strong> tuxedo jacket standing out among a sea of traditional black tuxedos.</p>

[12] The lapels
[13] <p>I did say details mattered!</p><p>They're easy to forget, but lapels are important too: by tradition, notch or shawl (rounded) lapels are preferred.</p><p>As for the material, we recommend satin or a slightly more textured fabric that keeps a subtle sheen.</p><p>Note: for this kind of suit, avoid a vent in your jacket.</p>
[14] The trousers
[15] <p>Now for the trousers: they should be <strong>simple</strong>, but ideally they should also have a <strong>satin stripe</strong> as a <strong>matching detail</strong>, running along the <strong>outside seam</strong>.</p><p>(<strong>PS</strong>: in case you were wondering, avoid a flashy belt with your trousers; I'd advise sticking to something understated and elegant.)</p>

[30] The "semi-formal" outfit
[31] <p>This is often the <strong>most requested</strong> outfit and the one that <strong>works everywhere</strong>! It is less formal than a tuxedo but still more polished than a casual style.</p>
[33] <p>Suits and blazers are essential to a semi-formal outfit. I generally advise going for <strong>dark colours</strong>: navy blue, charcoal grey and black are the most suitable. Look for <strong>well-fitted</strong>, tailored suits in simple single- or double-breasted styles. If your suit ticks all these boxes, you can be sure of making <strong>no mistakes</strong>.</p>
[36] <p>For the shirt, no risks: once again, stick to the <strong>classics</strong>. As for <strong>details</strong>, I'd recommend a <strong>cutaway or wing collar</strong> and <strong>rounded or French cuffs.</strong></p>

[47] The cocktail outfit
[48] <p>Now it's time for the cocktail style, often the <strong>most popular</strong> and requested today. This style is more <strong>relaxed</strong> but no less classy; it is simply less formal than the two we've seen above.</p>
[49] How to choose your cocktail outfit?
[50] <p>For a cocktail outfit, I'd advise choosing according to <strong>the season</strong>, <strong>the time of year</strong> and <strong>the style of the invitation.</strong></p><p>For example, if the wedding takes place in <strong>winter</strong>, I'd recommend a suit in heavier, warmer materials such as wool or velvet. Whereas if the wedding is in <strong>summer</strong>, lighter materials are more appropriate: wool blends, linen and cotton are pleasant and, above all, very handsome to wear at a spring or summer wedding.</p><p><strong>Colours</strong> also matter in the cocktail style: if the wedding takes place in autumn or winter, or in the evening, the most suitable colours are dark ones such as navy blue, forest green, grey or brown. For a spring or summer daytime wedding, pastel, nude and light colours are the way to go. Lately, beige, cream and pastel blue are often seen.</p><p><strong>The invitations</strong> can also guide you on colours: they usually match the wedding's theme. But if <strong>you're still unsure</strong>, you can always ask the <a href="https://lovelydayevent.fr/"><strong>wedding planner</strong></a> in charge of the wedding; she'll know exactly how to <strong>guide</strong> you.
