[2] Why hire a wedding planner in Bordeaux?

It also saves you precious time on the region’s specific logistics: château access, shuttles, guest accommodation, a rain plan… <a href="https://lovelydayevent.fr/pourquoi-organiser-son-mariage-en-region-bordelaise/">Getting married in the Bordeaux region</a> then becomes a pleasure, not an obstacle course.</p>

[16] Questions I am often asked
[17] When should you book your wedding planner?
[18] <p>For Signature or Collection, ideally 12 to 18 months before the date, when you start looking for your venue. For Sérénité, simply book before our kick-off meeting, four months before the wedding. Dates from May to September go quickly: it is best to get in touch early.</p>
