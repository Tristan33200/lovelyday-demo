[T] Behind the scenes with a wedding planner
[D] Go behind the scenes with a wedding planner in Nouvelle-Aquitaine: emotions, organisation and the secrets of magical events.
[0] <p>In the photos, everything looks simple: <strong>radiant newlyweds</strong>, <strong>perfect decor</strong>, a <strong>magical atmosphere</strong>.<br/>But behind this apparent ease lie <strong>months of preparation</strong>, dozens of suppliers to coordinate, and a wedding planner working behind the scenes to make sure every detail is exactly where it should be.</p><p>I am a <strong>wedding planner and designer in Nouvelle-Aquitaine</strong>, and today I want to take you <strong>backstage in my profession</strong>. Because while weddings are made of sparkle and love, they are also made of logistics, emotion and passion. Here is what you don't always see…</p>
[1] Before the big day: meticulous preparation
[2] <h3>First meetings with the couple</h3><p>It always starts with a meeting. A precious moment, often full of <strong>emotion</strong>, when two people entrust me with <strong>one of the most important days of their lives</strong>. This first conversation is much more than a simple appointment: it is the beginning of a human adventure, a timeless interlude in which we get to know each other.</p><p>Over a coffee, a phone call or a video call, the conversation flows naturally. We talk about their <strong>story</strong>, how they <strong>met</strong>, what brings them together. We laugh, sometimes we are moved, and very quickly we start <strong>dreaming together</strong>. These exchanges are essential: they help me understand who they are, what they love and, above all, what they want to feel on the <strong>big day</strong>.</p><p>This is often when the first <strong>ideas</strong> are born. A bohemian, authentic wedding in the heart of the Bordeaux vineyards, an intimate symbolic ceremony under the pines of the Arcachon Bay, or an elegant, refined reception in a château in the Périgord. Every detail begins to take shape, guided by their wishes, their personality and their vision of the wedding.</p><p>Every couple has their own world, their own codes, their own emotions. My role is to listen, to feel and to translate this uniqueness into a celebration that deeply resembles them. <strong>Turning their dreams</strong>, emotions and story into a <strong>unique and unforgettable experience</strong>: that is the heart of my work as a wedding planner.</p><h3>Finding suppliers and the logistical puzzle</h3><p>Once the wedding concept is <strong>approved</strong>, a key stage of the planning begins: finding and selecting the suppliers. Caterer, photographer, florist, DJ, decorator, officiant, stationery, accommodation… each professional plays an essential role in the success of the big day.</p><p>Behind this selection lie many hours of research, comparisons, meetings and conversations. But beyond technical skills, I pay <strong>particular attention to people</strong>. Because a successful wedding relies above all on a close-knit, caring team aligned around one shared vision: the couple's.</p><p>My role is to create real connections between the suppliers and the couple, so that everyone understands the world of the wedding and commits to it fully. This synergy ensures <strong>smooth organisation</strong>, <strong>effective communication</strong> and a <strong>calm atmosphere</strong> throughout the preparations.</p><p>Every element is planned down to the last detail: set-up times, deliveries, coordination on the day, and of course a plan B in case of bad weather. And respecting the budget remains at the heart of every decision, so I can offer the right solutions without ever compromising on <strong>quality</strong> or <strong>emotion</strong>.</p><p>It is this rigour, combined with a human approach, that turns a project into a <strong>harmonious, well-managed and unforgettable wedding</strong>.</p>
[5] <h3>The moodboard: the birth of the concept</h3><p>Before the first bouquets are delivered or the decor comes to life, everything starts with the <strong>moodboard</strong>. It is a deeply <strong>creative</strong> stage, a key moment when the <strong>wedding concept</strong> takes visual shape. I gather inspiration, colour palettes, textures, materials, plays of light and atmospheres to give the project a clear direction.</p><p>The moodboard is a true <strong>common thread</strong>. It helps the couple picture themselves there, feel the atmosphere of their day and imagine the world that will surround them. You could compare it to the trailer of the wedding to come: the soul of the project takes shape, <strong>emotions</strong> become images, and every detail gradually finds its place.</p><p>In <strong>Nouvelle-Aquitaine</strong>, the possibilities are endless and the styles extremely varied. A boho-chic wedding inspired by the Landes, an elegant, refined celebration in the heart of the Médoc vineyards, or a natural, pared-back wedding facing the ocean, lulled by the sound of the waves. Every territory and every landscape becomes a unique source of inspiration.</p><p>The moodboard then serves as the working basis for all the suppliers. It guarantees <strong>visual and emotional harmony</strong> and ensures that every element, from the flowers to the stationery, the furniture and the outfits, tells the same story: the couple's.</p>
[6] The days before: between excitement and precision
[7] <h3>The final adjustments</h3><p>Three days before, everything speeds up. The excitement builds and every detail becomes crucial. Suppliers confirm their arrival and set-up times, the last payments are made, and I go over the timeline again and again to make sure everything is perfectly orchestrated. It is a real <strong>race against the clock</strong>, where every minute counts.</p><p>The days get longer, and so do the evenings. I make late-night calls to adjust a timing, confirm a delivery or anticipate a problem. I check the stock of candles, decor items and floral arrangements. I prepare the emergency kits, the ones you hope never to use but that save the day when a detail slips out of control.</p><p><strong>Room layouts</strong>, <strong>seating plans</strong>, <strong>ceremony running order</strong>, <strong>final checklists</strong>… everything is scrutinised. I linger over the small details nobody really notices but that make all the difference on the <strong>big day</strong>: the ones that ensure the smooth flow, comfort and peace of mind of the couple and their guests.</p><p>My goal at this stage is clear: for the couple to be able to <strong>let go</strong> with complete confidence. Because when everything has been anticipated, checked and secured, they can enjoy every moment with a light heart while I make sure, behind the scenes, that their day runs smoothly.</p><h3>Set-up: the art of orchestrating everything</h3><p>The day before the wedding is often one of the most <strong>intense</strong> and <strong>moving</strong> moments of the whole adventure. This is when months of preparation finally come to life. With my <strong>team</strong>, we arrive at the venue, often first thing in the morning, laden with boxes, flowers, fabrics… and carefully imagined dreams.</p><p>Little by little, an empty space turns into a place full of <strong>emotion</strong>. Every element finds its place: a delicately tied ribbon, a precisely positioned candle, a carefully laid plate. Nothing is left to chance. Every detail helps tell a <strong>story</strong> and create a <strong>unique atmosphere</strong>.</p><p>This is when my role as a <strong>wedding designer</strong> comes fully into play. I play with volumes, natural or soft light, materials and textures to give depth to the whole. I adjust, move and refine until the <strong>harmony</strong> is perfect and true to the concept imagined with the couple.</p><p>Meanwhile, the couple can <strong>relax</strong> and savour the <strong>excitement</strong> of the hours to come. They know everything is in good hands. That is precisely why they chose me: to fully live these precious moments, without stress or pressure, while I handle every detail behind the scenes.</p>
[10] The big day: in the shadow of the spotlight
[11] <h3>Emotion at the heart of the job</h3><p>On the <strong>wedding day</strong>, I am <strong>everywhere… and nowhere at once</strong>. Present without being seen, attentive to every detail and every look. I anticipate, adjust, reassure, smile, fix things when needed and applaud in silence. My role is to make sure everything flows <strong>smoothly</strong>, without ever disturbing the <strong>magic</strong> of the moment.</p><p>In my bag there are always the essentials: pins, tape, tissues, a needle and thread, a backup plan and, above all, a lot of love. Because beyond the organisation and logistics, this job is lived with the heart.</p><p>The most powerful moments are often the simplest:</p><ul><li>When the bride puts on her dress and is overwhelmed with emotion.</li><li>When the guests see the decor for the first time, eyes shining.</li><li>When the music starts, silence falls, and everything suddenly becomes real.</li></ul><p>It is these <strong>suspended moments</strong>, full of <strong>emotion</strong>, that give my job its meaning. And at every wedding, I think the same thing, my heart full of gratitude:<br/><strong>“Yes, it was all worth it.”</strong></p><h3>The unexpected: my daily tightrope walk</h3><p>There is no such thing as a wedding without <strong>surprises</strong>. And that is probably what makes every event unique. Despite months of meticulous preparation, there is always that little grain of sand ready to upset the plan: a sudden shower that forces a rethink of the ceremony, a delayed supplier, a suit button that comes off at the last minute.</p><p>My job is then to walk a tightrope, in <strong>constant balance</strong>. Anticipate, react, decide quickly and well. Find an <strong>effective solution</strong> while keeping a smile, reassure the couple, and preserve the atmosphere and the emotion. These adjustments often happen behind the scenes, with the utmost discretion.</p><p>That is the beauty, and the challenge, of being a <strong>wedding planner</strong>: knowing how to turn the unexpected into an opportunity. A relocated ceremony becomes more intimate, a delay becomes a moment of sharing, a hastily fixed detail leaves nothing behind but a <strong>perfect memory</strong>.</p><p>And when nobody notices what almost went wrong, I know my <strong>mission is accomplished</strong>.</p>
