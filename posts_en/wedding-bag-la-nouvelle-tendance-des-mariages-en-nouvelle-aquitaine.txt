[T] The wedding bag: the new wedding trend in Nouvelle-Aquitaine
[D] The wedding bag is the trend of the Bordeaux region. Usually given as a gift to guests, it can also double as decoration.
[1] <p>The latest trend for your <strong>wedding</strong> in <strong>Nouvelle-Aquitaine</strong> is the <strong>wedding bag</strong>.</p><p>A perfect companion that will delight your guests.</p>
[a2] Trend
[3] A new trend!
[4] <p>Inspired by the welcome bag, the <strong>wedding bag</strong> is the ideal way to break with the tradition of the little pouch of sugared almonds as a gift for your wedding guests. Usually a tote bag, it is filled with small, minimalist gifts and plenty of accessories your guests might need during your <strong>wedding</strong>. A simple, eco-friendly way to please everyone.</p><p>Your bag can of course be personalised, for example with your initials and the date of the big day. It can also reflect your <strong>theme</strong>, whether <strong>chic</strong>, <strong>vintage</strong>, <strong>romantic</strong> or <strong>boho</strong>. The <strong>wedding bag</strong> is filled with lots of little items such as traditional sugared almonds, a fan against the heat, tissues for emotional moments, a detailed programme of the day for the impatient, or car <strong>decorations</strong> for the "car lovers".</p>
[5] Plenty of choice!
[6] <p>There are many options, and they can be designed in your image or that of your <strong>wedding</strong>. A great way to make sure the day stays engraved in your memory and your guests'. You can also slip in hand sanitiser or personalised face masks, a thoughtful touch when health is a concern.</p>
[7] A gift for the impatient
[8] <p>Handed out at a specific moment of your day, it can also help keep your guests entertained while they wait. That might be during the photo session or as soon as they arrive, just before your <strong>ceremony</strong>. Ideally, find the right moment so they can discover their <strong>wedding bag</strong> at leisure.</p>
