[0] <p>Choosing between an <strong>intimate wedding</strong> and a <strong>grand wedding</strong> is a crucial decision, and often a source of disagreement for couples.</p>
[a1] intimate
[2] An intimate wedding
[3] <p>An intimate wedding offers a <strong>warm, close-knit atmosphere</strong>, letting you share precious moments with a smaller number of guests, often family and close friends. It is often <strong>less stressful</strong> for the couple, as it is easier to organise and lets you enjoy each person in a unique, meaningful way. It will be easier to spend time with everyone who is there for you on this day of celebration. An intimate wedding also <strong>means fewer headaches</strong> and less preparation, given the smaller number of guests.</p><p><br/><br/><br/></p>
[4] A grand wedding
[5] <p>By contrast, a grand wedding brings together a <strong>large number of people</strong> for a more spectacular, grandiose celebration with a <strong>festive, electric atmosphere</strong>. It also lets the couple express their style and personality through a <strong>memorable, entertaining atmosphere</strong>, with, for example, a large, beautiful dance floor, extravagant, sumptuous decorations and an extraordinary venue! On the other hand, it can be more <strong>complex to organise</strong> on your own in terms of logistics, coordination and budget.</p>
