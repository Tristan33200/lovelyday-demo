[40] How to create your perfect colour moodboard
[41] <p>Creating a <strong>colour moodboard</strong> is an essential step in planning and designing your wedding. It is a visual tool that gathers and harmonises every element of your decor to achieve a coherent, elegant atmosphere. With it, you can see the balance between the <strong>stationery</strong>, the <strong>flowers</strong>, the <strong>tableware</strong>, the <strong>outfits</strong>, the <strong>decor</strong> and the <strong>lighting</strong> before you even finalise your choices.</p><p>A well-designed moodboard makes communication easier with your wedding planner, your florists, your decorators and every supplier involved. [42] A professional method in 5 steps
[43] <ol><li><strong>Define the main emotion</strong>: start by identifying the atmosphere you want to convey: romantic, festive, minimalist or warm. This emotion will guide all your colour decisions.</li><li><strong>Choose a main colour</strong>: select the shade that will be the common thread of your wedding and will appear in the key elements of the decor.</li><li><strong>Add 2 or 3 complementary colours</strong>: these shades will enrich your palette, create subtle contrasts and strengthen the visual harmony.</li><li><strong>Bring in a texture</strong>: pair your colours with specific materials such as linen, velvet, wood or metal. These textures add depth and character to your decor.</li><li><strong>Test the coherence in natural light</strong>: look at your palette at different times of day to make sure the colours stay harmonious whatever the light, soft or bright.</li></ol><p>By following this method, you can design a balanced, elegant <strong>colour moodboard</strong> that is true to your vision, so that every detail of your wedding contributes to a harmonious, memorable scenography.</p>
