[2] A personalised wedding: much more than a trend
[3] <p>The personalised wedding isn't a passing fad. It is a real <strong>evolution</strong> in the way we <strong>celebrate love and commitment</strong>. [5] Made-to-measure wedding outfits: expressing your identity
[6] <p>Wedding outfits are becoming true <strong>means of personal expression</strong>. In 2026, couples want to wear creations that tell their story rather than simply follow trends.</p><p><strong>Personalising</strong> outfits comes through the choice of cuts, fabrics and details. [10] Handcrafted invitations: creating the first memory
[11] <p>The personalised experience begins as soon as the wedding is announced. The invitation is the first <strong>emotional contact</strong> with guests. It sets the tone, conveys an atmosphere and builds anticipation.</p><p><strong>Handcrafted</strong> invitations are enjoying a real revival. Combining paper and digital strengthens the personalisation without diluting it.</p>
