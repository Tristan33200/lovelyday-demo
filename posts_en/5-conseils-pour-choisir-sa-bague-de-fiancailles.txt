[14] Understanding the 4 Cs of diamonds: carat, clarity, colour and cut
[15] Carat
[16] <p>A diamond's <strong>carat</strong> refers to its weight, which directly affects its size and cost. It is crucial to strike a balance between the size of the diamond and its overall quality, according to <strong>your budget</strong>. A larger diamond isn't always the best choice if its quality compromises brilliance and purity.</p>
[18] Clarity
[20] <p>A diamond's <strong>clarity</strong> measures the presence of inclusions or imperfections visible to the naked eye. High-clarity diamonds are rarer and more expensive, but diamonds with minor inclusions can offer equivalent beauty at a lower cost. The important thing is to choose a diamond whose inclusions don't significantly affect its beauty.</p>
[21] Colour
[22] <p>Diamonds are graded on a <strong>colour</strong> scale from D (colourless) to Z (tinted yellow or brown). Colourless diamonds (D–F) are the most prized for their purity and brilliance. However, diamonds with a slight tint (G–J) can also be very beautiful while being more affordable.</p>
[24] Cut
[26] <p>A diamond's <strong>cut</strong> refers not only to its physical shape, but also to the way it is cut to maximise its brilliance and the dispersion of light. A well-executed cut can turn a diamond of average quality into a sparkling, attractive stone.</p>
[27] Which metal should you choose for the band?
[28] <p>The <strong>choice of metal</strong> for the <strong>engagement ring</strong> determines not only its look, but also its durability and long-term care.</p>
[29] White, yellow or rose gold:
[30] <p>White gold offers a modern, elegant look, perfect for those who appreciate simplicity and sophistication. Yellow gold, classic and warm, evokes timeless tradition. Rose gold, romantic and on-trend, is ideal for those looking for a touch of modernity in their choice of ring.</p>
[32] Platinum:
[33] <p>Platinum is a precious metal known for its exceptional durability and scratch resistance. Although more expensive than gold, platinum is hypoallergenic, making it an ideal option for those with sensitive skin who want a long-lasting, low-maintenance ring.</p>
[35] Silver:
[36] <p>Less expensive than gold and platinum, silver is an affordable alternative for an engagement ring. However, it needs regular care to prevent tarnishing and keep its shine over time.</p>
