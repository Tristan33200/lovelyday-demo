[7] -Get a massage-
[8] <p>To keep stress at bay, it is important to take <strong>time for yourself</strong> and relax. A massage will give you a break from the preparations. A relaxing massage can help you unwind and improve your emotional wellbeing. Massages can even stimulate the release of endorphins, the "happiness hormones", which can help steady your mood.</p>
[9] -Spend time together-
[10] <p>It may seem trivial, but spending time together and <strong>reconnecting</strong> is essential to help you approach the wedding day with <strong>serenity</strong> and <strong>joy</strong>. Whatever activity you choose, such as a picnic in Bordeaux's Parc Bordelais, the key is to create a relaxing space where you can connect, laugh and focus on the <strong>love</strong> and <strong>commitment</strong> ahead.</p>
[13] -Aromatherapy-
[14] <p><strong>The big day</strong> is often the most stressful moment of all the preparations, even when everything is perfectly organised. Rest assured, you're not alone!</p><p>Soothing yourself with scents is one way to stay calm on the big day. Aromatherapy is a method that has shown convincing results. Essential oils, extracted from plants, can have relaxing and calming properties. A few drops of essential oil applied to the skin or diluted in a diffuser, and calm is guaranteed!</p>
[15] -Breathing exercises-
[16] <p>We often hear about breathing exercises because they have proved their worth.</p><p>The key is to choose an exercise that suits you best and can easily be built into your routine on the wedding day. Breathing exercises can help reduce <strong>stress</strong>, calm the nerves and create a calm, centred state of mind so you can fully <strong>enjoy</strong> this special day.</p>
[19] -Treat yourselves-
[20] <p>And above all, don't forget to enjoy the present moment. This event will only happen once in your life, so live it to the full without putting pressure on yourselves or worrying about what others think. This day is yours and it should be 100% you.</p><p>Why not a little glass of champagne to help you <strong>relax</strong>? No one will hold it against you!</p>
